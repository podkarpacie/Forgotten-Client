Music files were removed from this distribution.

The client previously shipped with a startup music track (startup.ogg) here.
It has been removed for licensing reasons.

To add your own startup music:
  1. Obtain a music file you have the rights to use (Ogg Vorbis format,
     named startup.ogg).
  2. Place it in this folder (data/sounds/).
  3. Restart the client - it will play automatically at the login screen.

The startup track is referenced in modules/client/client.lua
(musicFilename = "/sounds/startup").
